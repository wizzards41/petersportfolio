/VA Skills

Sample jobs a client might give me, with the problem, what I would do, and the result.

📅 Schedule & Meeting Management

The Problem

A client has meetings all over their emails and chats. They get double-booked, miss calls, and waste time going back and forth to find a free time.

The Process I Did

  1. Got access to the client's calendar and asked for their preferred hours
  2. Collected all meetings into one Google Calendar
  3. Fixed the double-booked meetings
  4. Blocked time for breaks and focus work
  5. Set reminders and sent a weekly schedule summary

The Result

A clean calendar with no conflicts. The client always knows what is next and shows up prepared and on time.

📧 Email & Inbox Management

The Problem

A client's inbox has hundreds of unread emails. Important messages from customers get buried and replies are always late.

The Process I Did

  1. Created labels (Urgent, Clients, Invoices, Later)
  2. Set up filters to sort new emails automatically
  3. Unsubscribed from junk and archived old emails
  4. Wrote reply drafts for the client to approve
  5. Flagged emails that needed the client's attention

The Result

An organized inbox where urgent emails stand out. Replies go out faster and nothing important is missed.

📊 Data Entry & Spreadsheets

The Problem

A client keeps customer orders and contacts in notes, photos, and messy files. It is hard to find information or see how the business is doing.

The Process I Did

  1. Collected all the raw information from the client
  2. Designed a clear spreadsheet with proper columns
  3. Entered the data and double-checked for mistakes
  4. Added filters and formulas for totals and searching
  5. Shared the file and showed how to update it

The Result

One accurate, searchable spreadsheet. The client can find any record in seconds and track sales easily.
